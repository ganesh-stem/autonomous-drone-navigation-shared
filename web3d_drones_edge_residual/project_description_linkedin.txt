🚁 Autonomous Drone: Residual SAC on a PID Baseline
==============

A drone trained with reinforcement learning, but instead of learning the whole flight policy on its own, it only has to learn a correction on top of a fixed, hand-written PID controller. It flies through procedurally generated towers across five weather conditions, from calm air to a dusty gale.


Training Results
===========

🔹 747,520 flight steps
🔹 373,247 gradient steps
🔹 99.8% of 1,585 attempts reached the goal
🔹 1,582 goals reached
🔹 5.9 collisions per 1,000 steps (averaged across all 5 weather conditions)
🔹 8.8 goals per 1,000 steps (averaged across all 5 weather conditions)
🔹 Final average reward score: 0.41


After that training run, the final weights were frozen and tested live. The video below shows that test, with the model computing a fresh correction on top of the PID baseline at every step as the weather turns.
